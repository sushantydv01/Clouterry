# Clouterry Design Directives
All code and UI development MUST strictly follow the directives in [DESIGN_BRIEF.md](./DESIGN_BRIEF.md):
- **Planet as Main Character**: Restraint everywhere except the 3D planet. The planet has personality ("spinning because it wants to, a little unhinged").
- **No Stock Photography**: Zero stock photos anywhere on the site. Visual weight carried 100% by typography, planet, and brand geometry.
- **Form Directly on Red**: Bare underlined inputs directly on full-bleed red background (no floating white cards).
- **Shape Language**: Crisp rectangular button geometry (`rounded-md` / `rounded-sm`), NOT generic `rounded-full` everywhere.
- **Restraint & Tone Fork**: Creator section is bold, loose, uncomfortable-confident full-bleed red. Brand section is quiet, restrained, cream/ink/hairline rule. Gold is genuinely rare (max 1–2 uses on the entire site).
- **Typography**: Big headlines, hand-set `<br/>` pauses, no single word colored differently, no tracked-out small caps eyebrow badges.
