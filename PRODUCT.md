# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript

## Users

Brands, schools and educational institutions, startups, small to mid-sized businesses, professional practices, and organizations seeking an enduring, coherent digital presence.

## Product Purpose

FIRM is a Digital Presence & Growth Firm. We engineer enduring digital presences for ambitious organizations, unifying brand identity, bespoke Next.js platforms, documentary editorial content, and systematic visibility under one disciplined architectural standard.

## Positioning

Not an agency of disconnected sub-contractors, nor a generic SaaS template shop. An accountable, integrated architectural stewardship executing senior design craft, modern software engineering, and disciplined visibility under a single standard with zero fabricated claims.

## Operating Context

Commercial organizations, institutional admissions, corporate leadership, and prospective partners evaluating digital capabilities, retrospective case studies, field insights, and engaging through structured inquiry briefings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 5 core divisions: Brand & Identity, Web & Technology, Social Media & Content, Marketing & Growth, Digital Management & Consulting.
- 5 industry sectors: Education, Healthcare, Professional Services, Retail, Startups.
- Technical stack: Next.js 16.3.4 (Turbopack), React 19.2.8, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript 5, Netlify deployment.
- High performance: Static/SSG prerendering for all content routes, zero JS overhead for static reading, sub-600ms builds.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1), responsive layout across 360px–1440px viewports, zero horizontal overflow.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: FIRM
- Visual identity: Cheerful Neo-Brutalism (Gumroad-inspired: bold, flat, playful, confident, solid 1px black outlines, candy-coloured surfaces, Space Grotesk typography)
- Distinctive architectural keystone mark and bold uppercase wordmark.
- Strictly human Git authorship (Sushant Yadav).

## Evidence on Hand

- 3 comprehensive case retrospectives: Saint Jude Academy (`/work/saint-jude-academy`), Aether Health (`/work/aether-health`), Vance Legal (`/work/vance-legal`).
- 6 complete editorial monographs (`src/data/articles.ts`).
- 13 custom art-directed schemas, visual previews, and diagrams (`public/images/`).
- Interactive Presence Matrix and School Digital Blueprint tools.

## Product Principles

1. Cohesion over fragmentation: Unify brand, code, and distribution.
2. Honesty in representation: Real retrospective analysis; zero fabricated metrics or fictional testimonials.
3. Performance and accessibility as commercial prerequisites, not afterthoughts.
4. Editorial depth: Demonstrate deep industry knowledge through authoritative monographs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, semantic HTML5, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, responsive fluid scaling, prefers-reduced-motion accommodations.
